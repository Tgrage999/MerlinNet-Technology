BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Technology services: your questions answered

The essentials about getting help from MerlinNet Technology.

Discuss your needs

Who are MerlinNet’s services for?

MerlinNet Technology serves the general public. You can enquire about personal technology needs, computer training, websites, or a custom digital project.

Are services available remotely or in person?

Both remote and in-person services are available. Email us with your location and the help you need to confirm suitable arrangements before planning a visit or appointment.

How much do services cost?

Costs depend on your requirements. Describe the support, training, website, or software you need when you enquire so we can discuss a tailored quotation.

Can I ask about basic computer training?

Yes. MerlinNet offers computer literacy and practical digital-skills training. Tell us your current experience and what you want to learn when asking about training.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include the service you need, the problem or goal, and whether you prefer remote or in-person help. For projects, include any requirements, budget, or target date you already have. Do not send passwords or sensitive account details.

Does the enquiry form send my message automatically?

No. It prepares an email in your email application; you must send it there. If no application opens, email merlinnettechnology@gmail.com directly.
